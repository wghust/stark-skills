# Skill Power Archetypes

Use this reference only when naming titles or explaining score bands.

## Score Bands

| Omnipotence | Rank | Chinese label | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 90-100 | SSS | 全域支配者 | Broad, deep, balanced, and operational. |
| 85-89 | SS | 万能勇者 | Nearly complete toolkit with a few thin edges. |
| 75-84 | S | 大师阶 | Strong, recognizable specialty and usable breadth. |
| 60-74 | A | 专精阶 | Dominant in one or two domains; not all-purpose yet. |
| 40-59 | B | 见习阶 | Useful but uneven or sparse. |
| 0-39 | C | 初始村冒险者 | Too few skills or weak metadata. |

## Combination Titles

Use combination titles when the top two domains are close in count or score:

- `frontend` + `image-design`: 界面织法者.
- `docs-writing` + `research-analysis`: 洞察书记官.
- `backend-devops` + `git-collab`: 工程战线指挥官.
- `automation-tools` + `knowledge-memory`: 自走知识机关师.
- `business-product` + `research-analysis`: 战略预言官.
- Three or more strong domains with high balance: 全栈炼金术士.

## Tone Control

- `normal`: Use one playful title, keep the rest plain.
- `dramatic`: Use rank labels, title, and short fantasy metaphors.
- `full-chuunibyou`: Add stronger wording, but keep evidence and scores factual. Do not insult the user or make unverifiable claims.
